Coverage and limits

Locomotion, eight-direction walk/jog/run and crouch, sprint, crawling, carrying, jumps, transitions, combat, reactions, get-ups, gestures and traversal probes. Swimming, weapon rigs, vehicles, paired combat and magic remain in the checklist backlog. Space selects vault/mantle prototypes on explicitly tagged barriers/walls with free landing room. The library retains full raw-length previews. Ladder and seat alignment, precise traversal hand contacts and height-specific pose adaptation still need work.

Camera-relative exploration. The character faces travel. Hold RMB for camera-heading focus and strafing; this is not held guard or target lock.

Speed-sensitive jump; near a marked low barrier, vault it; face a reachable tagged wall to climb onto it. Alt gives a short directional evade. New movement, C, attacks and Space cancel evade immediately.
C · Z

E
Context prompt: put down carried box, pick up nearby box, use terminal, sit/stand at bench. Seat alignment remains a contact probe.
LMB · F · B
Jab/cross · kick · one-shot block. Every click starts or restarts an attack, including the first click that captures the mouse. Space preempts attacks. Fresh WASD input cancels them.
Q / MMB · V
Recenter camera (target lock pending) · swap focused camera shoulder.
R · Shift+R

Start at the traversal yard: face the blue wall and press Space to climb onto it. The gold barrier beyond it is vaultable. Ctrl+Space requests a normal high jump. With the viewport focused, Ctrl+S walks backward and Ctrl+A/D walk sideways while facing the camera heading. These chords suppress browser Save / Select All / Bookmark only in the focused game. Other Ctrl/Command shortcuts do not trigger game actions; use C without Ctrl to crouch. Walls must be close, faced, reachable and have free standing room; perimeter walls and tunnel roofs are excluded. These authored paths use collision checks, not a learned physical climbing policy. Exact hand/foot contacts remain prototypes.

High ledge trial: face the purple 2.65 m platform and press Space. The move jumps, catches, hangs, pulls up and settles on top. Walk off to trigger a superhero landing after a sufficiently high fall. Superhero drop trial starts above the clear landing pad; replay either trial from the footer. Small hops use normal landings. The hero pose uses right knee down, left fist grounded and right elbow raised, then returns to standing; movement, C, attacks and Space can interrupt it.

These two moves add authored contact poses and controller timing to the existing Kimodo sources; no new neural inference is running. Forward trip trial: walk/run into a low hurdle using W. Backward trip trial: hold Ctrl+S to backpedal (this trial also enables Strafe). Low grounded solids (12–70 cm) can trigger a direction-appropriate ukemi when there is rollout space. Jumping, crouching, carrying and high-wall contacts do not trigger it. The falling/rolling part is committed; C, jump or another action can interrupt the upright recovery. A cooldown and separation check prevent one obstacle from retriggering continuously.

Hold Ctrl+A or Ctrl+D in the side trials to strafe into a low hurdle: the robot performs a sideways breakfall and rolls back to standing. Each side has three new Kimodo takes: two judo-style seeds and one skateboard-style shoulder roll. Variety chooses once when the reaction starts; both compared bodies use that seed. Low hurdles continue into clear rollout space; tall walls redirect the fall away from the wall, using the opposite side pool. Blocked routes do not launch a roll.
