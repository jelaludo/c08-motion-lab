Coverage and limits

Locomotion, eight-direction walk/jog/run and crouch, sprint, crawling, carrying, jumps, transitions, combat, reactions, get-ups, gestures and traversal probes. Swimming, weapon rigs, vehicles, paired combat and magic remain in the checklist backlog. Space selects vault/mantle prototypes on explicitly tagged barriers/walls with free landing room. The library retains full raw-length previews. Ladder and seat alignment, precise traversal hand contacts and height-specific pose adaptation still need work.

Game clips are separate in-place derivatives. They use loop phase search, an overlap seam blend and light smoothing. The live controller supplies travel, jump arcs, collisions, transitions and bounded foot/hand IK. Playground controls

W A S D
Move relative to the camera; combine for diagonals.
Right mouse drag / Mouse look
Orbit camera; Mouse look captures the pointer. Esc releases it. Mouse wheel zooms.
1 / 2 / 3 / 4 · Shift
Walk / jog / run / sprint. Hold Shift for sprint; Caps Lock toggles walking.
C · Z
Single C toggles crouch locomotion immediately; quick C C holds a static kneeling/seiza pose. Z toggles prone. Space jumps from crouch or seiza when headroom allows.
Space · Q
Jump from current speed and direction · dodge in the held direction.
E
Pick up a nearby box or put it down. Carrying uses dedicated idle, walk and jog clips plus hand IK.
Left mouse · F · B
Jab/cross · front kick · block. Hold right mouse to look while moving.
G · X · V
Use nearby terminal · sit/stand at bench · wave.
H · K · J
Hit reaction · knockdown · get up.
R · P · 0
Record/stop (120 seconds maximum) · pause · reset to start.
WASD + mouse
Camera-relative exploration. The character faces travel. Hold RMB for camera-heading focus and strafing; this is not held guard or target lock.
Shift · Caps Lock · 1-4
Sprint (Hold or Toggle below) · walk/jog toggle · lab speed overrides.
Space · Left Alt
Speed-sensitive jump; near a marked low barrier, vault it; face a reachable tagged wall to climb onto it. Alt gives a short directional evade. New movement, C, attacks and Space cancel evade immediately.
C · Z
C toggles crouch locomotion immediately; quick C C holds kneeling/seiza. WASD leaves seiza into crouch. Z toggles prone. Space immediately jumps from crouch/seiza where headroom allows.
E
Context prompt: put down carried box, pick up nearby box, use terminal, sit/stand at bench. Seat alignment remains a contact probe.
LMB · F · B
Jab/cross · kick · one-shot block. Every click starts or restarts an attack, including the first click that captures the mouse. Space preempts attacks. Fresh WASD input cancels them.
Q / MMB · V
Recenter camera (target lock pending) · swap focused camera shoulder.
R · Shift+R
R reserved for reload; unarmed lab has no reload. Shift+R records/stops (or use Record button).
Tab · Esc · P · 0
Motion library · pause/release mouse · pause/resume · reset.

Start at the traversal yard: face the blue wall and press Space to climb onto it. The gold barrier beyond it is vaultable. Ctrl+Space requests a normal high jump. Other Ctrl/Command browser shortcuts do not trigger game actions; use C without Ctrl to crouch. Walls must be close, faced, reachable and have free standing room; perimeter walls and tunnel roofs are excluded. These authored paths use collision checks, not a learned physical climbing policy. Exact hand/foot contacts remain prototypes.

The large course has an orange run lane followed by a red sprint lane, low jump barriers, a crouch tunnel, solid walls, a box yard, a bench and a terminal. Solid obstacles use a swept substep capsule approximation; the camera also avoids walls. The tour demonstrates connected inputs and actions.

Collisions and jumps are game-controller behavior. These are candidates for visual review, not a certified production animation library.
