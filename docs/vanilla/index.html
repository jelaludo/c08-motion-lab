How to review

1 = unusable, 3 = workable with fixes, 5 = ready for this lab. Leave a score blank if you cannot assess it. Look at foot sliding, armor overlap, weight, timing and entry/exit poses. Keep is your recommendation, not an automatic quality certificate.

Loops should meet their own starting pose. One-shots need a clear destination: sitting ends seated, lying down ends on the floor. They do not all return to standing. Transitions here are conventional sampled animation and pose blends; no new neural prediction is claimed.

The set is a practical selection, not a statistical ranking. Backward roll, modified jumps, floor recovery and blended boundaries need special scrutiny. There is no contact IK or collision solver. C08 only in this first review set.

Space: play/pause; arrows: previous/next (outside form fields). Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Capture the time of a defect in your notes.
