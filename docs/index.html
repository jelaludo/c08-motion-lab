A SMALL EXPERIMENT IN MOVEMENT

Good company.
Interesting motion.

C08 is a heavy utility robot. K0-Tech is his compact mechanical cat. This is a non-commercial, independently made motion experiment, shared to learn what feels alive—and what still needs work.

The worker’s generated actions use original G1 conditioning poses with NVIDIA MotionBricks. The cat’s eight actions are original authored animation. The diorama adds direction, crossfades, foot-clearance correction and deliberately staged interactions. It is kinematic choreography, not a physics simulation.

Watch the details

Does the worker feel heavy? Do the feet stay grounded? Do the changes between actions feel natural? Are the jumps convincing at these very different scales?

The cat’s leap over C08 adds an authored lift to its existing pounce. It demonstrates a staged interaction, rather than claiming that MotionBricks animates cats. Remaining joint-limit and contact findings are visible in the motion inspector. Its Test Gallery now includes six video-derived exercises, all five original C08 animations, and a shuffled Continuous playback mode (47 choices total).
