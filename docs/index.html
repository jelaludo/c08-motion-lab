WHAT’S DOING THE WORK?

Generation, playback,
and capture are different tools.

MB-Predictive demonstrates learned in-betweening from recent and target poses. Quaternius uses conventional animation clips and transitions. Video capture estimates a recorded performance. The diorama brings finished motions together through choreography.

The public predictive examples were generated on a local RTX 4090. Fresh AI generation is available through the local MB-Predictive launcher. The website itself is a static, shareable showcase.
