Reading this gallery

Reference: input/context video. H3 animation is not measured ground truth.

Control: a known rig/action rendered with known cameras, when its actual render is posted.

Recovery: an estimated reading/fitted motion.

Ablation: a stated processing change with other inputs held fixed where possible.

Queued/running means unfinished. Blocked/failed records a limitation or error. Complete means a job ended; review is still required.
