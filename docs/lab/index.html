What does ? mean?

A flag marks review findings, such as joint limits, large grounding corrections, uncertain recovery or contact drift. Read the findings above for the selected clip. Files can pass structural validation while still showing motion problems.

Reference skeletons retain their own scale. Individual motions and playlist blends are experiments; they are not a finished animation controller.

Where are the earlier reference tests?

The earlier local evaluation includes 24 clips conditioned on NVIDIA/Bones samples whose terms limit them to internal evaluation. They remain in the local lab.
